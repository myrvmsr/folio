## Short description

## Description

Folio is a free Markdown reader and editor for your local notes and documents. Open a file, enjoy a clear reading view, then switch to editing with a live preview. Your documents stay on your computer, and you can work offline with local files.

Bring several folders together in one panel, open documents in tabs and organize your work into separate spaces. Create, rename and move files from the application. Auto save and session restoration help you pick up where you left off.

Folio displays tables, highlighted code, mathematical formulas, Mermaid diagrams, task lists, footnotes and YAML metadata. An outline makes long documents easier to navigate. Export your notes to PDF or print them.

Choose a light, dark or automatic theme, customize colors and fonts, and adapt keyboard shortcuts. The interface is available in French, English, Spanish, German, Dutch, Italian and Portuguese.

No Folio account is required. Remote images or media in your documents and dictionary downloads may require an Internet connection.

## Features

- Markdown reading and editing with a live preview.
- Horizontal or vertical tabs and workspaces.
- Multiple folders and auto save.
- Mathematical formulas, Mermaid diagrams and code highlighting.
- Find and replace, with an optional spell checker.
- Customizable themes, colors, fonts and keyboard shortcuts.
- PDF export and printing.
- Seven interface languages, with no Folio account required.
