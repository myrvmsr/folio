# Code of conduct

Folio aims to be a calm place to read and write. Its community should feel the same: friendly, patient and welcoming to everyone, whatever their experience, background, identity or language.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intentions.
- Welcome newcomers and questions of every level. Everyone started somewhere.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person, and accept feedback gracefully.
- Respect people's time, privacy and different points of view.
- Keep discussions focused on making Folio better.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, personal attacks, mockery or trolling.
- Harassment of any kind, including repeated unwanted contact.
- Discriminatory jokes or language, and sexualized content.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any other behavior that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a friendly, public space.

## Where this applies

This code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and every other space of the Folio project, and to anyone who represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting a problem

If someone's behavior bothers you, you can mention [@myrvmsr](https://github.com/myrvmsr) in the conversation. To report it privately, open the **⋯** menu of the comment, issue or pull request, choose **Report content** and send it to the repository maintainers. You can also report it to GitHub from the same menu.

Every report is read and handled with discretion.

## Consequences

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock a conversation, or block an account temporarily or permanently, depending on the situation. Usually, a short reminder of this code is enough.
